Low-latency desktop & game streaming for LG webOS

  • Up to 4K120 with HDR. Hardware HEVC or H.264 decode straight on the TV's media pipeline — no browser, no transcode in the middle.
  • Adaptive bitrate. Leave it on Automatic and the stream tracks your link as it changes, backing off on loss and climbing again when it clears — or pin it anywhere from 10 to 200 Mbps.
  • Per-game settings. Hold a game's cover to give it its own resolution, frame rate, bitrate, codec, HDR, audio or controller.
  • Your library, on the TV. Games are listed with their cover art and launch with one press; pin the ones you play to the top row.
  • Surround audio. Stereo, 5.1 or 7.1, decoded client-side.
  • Every input the TV has. Magic Remote pointer, gamepads, USB keyboard and mouse — with pointer capture for games and absolute pointing for the desktop.
  • The full DualSense. Adaptive triggers, lightbar and player LEDs driven by the game, plus the touchpad, the motion sensors for gyro aiming, and rumble. Connect the pad over Bluetooth; on older webOS versions whose kernel predates the PlayStation driver, some features are limited.
  • Game mode. An optional Experimental setting on rooted TVs: it switches picture and sound to Game mode for the lowest input lag while streaming, then puts your previous settings back on exit.

Works on webOS 5 and later, with a fallback decode path for webOS 3.5–4.x.

Needs a punktfunk host running on your PC — see the host setup guide to get started.
